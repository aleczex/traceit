=== APP PURPOSE IN RELATION TO LOCATION PERMISSIONS ===
The core purpose of TraceIt is to give parents peace of mind by automatically monitoring children's school bus commutes. To achieve this, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION capture precise and approximate GPS positions to map routes and calculate distance/duration. ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION enables autonomous trip tracking in the background during scheduled school hours and geofences, recording accurate GPX tracks without requiring parents to keep the app open.

=== GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE LOCATION PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION (Under 500 chars) ===
TraceIt tracks school bus commutes to help parents verify safe routes. ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION provide precise and approximate GPS coordinates to calculate trip distance, plot routes on maps, and detect geofences. ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION is required for autonomous trip recording during scheduled school hours without keeping the app open, using a foreground service and activity recognition to log GPS points into GPX files safely.

=== BACKGROUND LOCATION FEATURE DESCRIPTION (Under 500 chars) ===
Automated School Bus Route Tracking: The app features smart scheduling and geofencing that automatically tracks a child's school bus commute in the background. Parents set home/school geofences and active school hours. When the device departs home during scheduled times, a foreground service autonomously records GPS route points into GPX files, calculates distance and duration statistics, and stops upon arrival—all without requiring the user to keep the app open.
